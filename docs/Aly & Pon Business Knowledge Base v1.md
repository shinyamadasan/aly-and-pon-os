## Summary

The Aly & Pon Business Knowledge Base is a Notion-based business knowledge system. It preserves brand strategy, product positioning, content knowledge, and business decisions without becoming an operational system.

Guiding rule: **The app runs the business. Notion explains the business. Git preserves the system.**

## Structure

### Home

- Mission
- Quick Links
- Current Priorities
- Recent Product Catalog entries
- Recent Decision Log entries
- How to use this knowledge base

### Brand

- Brand Bible
- Inspiration
- Visual Identity
- Packaging

The Brand Bible remains one canonical document and should not be split into many pages.

Brand Bible metadata:

- Version: 1.0
- Last Updated: 2026-07-28
- Owner: Aly & Pon

### Product Catalog

Purpose: brand-facing product documentation.  
Business owner: Product

Properties:

- Product

Inside each product page:

- Story
- Selling Points

### Decision Log

## Business Ownership

| Area | Business Owner |
| --- | --- |
| Brand | Brand |
| Product Catalog | Product |
| Content Library | Marketing |
| Decision Log | Business |

Ownership defines responsibility for meaning and quality, not a specific person.

## Relationships

- Content Library -> Product Catalog through `Related Product`
- Decision Log -> Product Catalog through `Related Product`
- Decision Log -> Content Library through `Related Content`

These relationships exist for context and memory, not workflow management.

## Explicit Exclusions

Do not add these to v1:

- CRM
- Finance
- Inventory
- Recipes
- Suppliers
- Projects
- Tasks
- Operations
- Website CMS
- SEO management
- Employee management
- Production
